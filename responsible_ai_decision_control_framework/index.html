Control rules

High-impact decision: autonomous approval prohibited; authorised human review required.
Confidence below threshold: create exception reason and route for review/escalation.
Policy/control failure: stop progression and record failed rule, owner and resolution.
Every outcome: retain decision ID, inputs, model/rule version, reviewer, rationale and timestamp.
